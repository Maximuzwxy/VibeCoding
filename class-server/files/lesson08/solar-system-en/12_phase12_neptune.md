# Phase 12 — Neptune + Great Dark Spot + Triton Retrograde Orbit

## Goal
Implement Neptune, the last planet, validating deep blue ice giant texture and single moon with retrograde orbit.

## Requirements

### Neptune Specifics
- Deep blue surface texture (methane-rich atmosphere)
- Great Dark Spot: prominent dark storm feature
- Bright white storm clouds
- Axial tilt: 28.32°
- Semi-transparent atmosphere

### Triton
- One moon with retrograde (clockwise) orbit
- Pinkish-grey surface
- Largest of Neptune's moons
