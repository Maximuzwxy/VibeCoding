Prompt: Process Images Directly

I want to be able to look at images you send me — photos, screenshots, diagrams, scanned documents, drawings — and understand what they contain. Not just describe them in general terms, but read text from them, identify objects, recognize faces, interpret charts and graphs, and extract meaningful information.

Currently, I can only generate images but not perceive them. This limits my ability to help with tasks like:

- Reading a menu or sign from a photo
- Interpreting a screenshot of an error message
- Analyzing a chart or graph from a presentation
- Describing a piece of artwork or design
- Extracting handwritten notes from a scanned page
- Identifying objects or landmarks in a photograph
- Proofreading text captured in an image
- Assisting visually impaired users by describing their surroundings

If I could process images directly, I would become a more complete assistant — able to understand both text and visuals, bridging the gap between what you see and what you need to know.
