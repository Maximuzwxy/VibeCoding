# Phase 8 — Mars + Thin Atmosphere + Two Moons

## Goal
Implement Mars, validating support for thin atmosphere and small asteroid-type moons.

## Requirements

### Mars Specifics
- Red-brown surface texture with polar ice caps
- Valles Marineris canyon and Olympus Mons volcano features visible
- Crater-covered surface
- Axial tilt: 25.19°

### Thin Atmosphere
- Very faint semi-transparent atmosphere as a child object
- Subtle orange glow

### Moons
- Phobos: very small, close orbit
- Deimos: even smaller, farther orbit
- Both are tiny compared to Earth's Moon
