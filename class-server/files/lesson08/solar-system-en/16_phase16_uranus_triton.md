# Phase 16 — Uranus Moons (5) + Triton

## Goal
Implement close-up textures for Uranus's five major moons and Neptune's Triton.

## Requirements

### Uranus Moons (5)

| Moon | Visual Features | Color |
|------|----------------|-------|
| **Miranda** | Grey ice-rock + chevron terrain + craters | Grey |
| **Ariel** | Bright grey ice + canyon fractures + bright spots | Light grey |
| **Umbriel** | Very dark grey + few bright crater rims | Dark grey |
| **Titania** | Mid grey (largest Uranian moon) + craters | Mid grey |
| **Oberon** | Dark grey + ancient impact surface | Dark grey |

### Triton (Neptune's Moon)
- Pinkish-grey cantaloupe terrain
- Nitrogen ice plumes
- Polar ice cap

- Most Uranian moons share a common ice-moon texture generator
- Triton has its own unique texture
