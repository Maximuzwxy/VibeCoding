📖 Game Rules

🎮 Game Objective

Pilot your spaceship through 6 dangerous levels, defeat enemy forces, and survive boss battles to become the ultimate space ace!

⌨️ Controls

↑ ↓ ← → Move spaceship
SPACE Fire bullets
P Pause game (Single Player only)

2 Player Mode: Player 1 uses Arrow Keys, Player 2 uses W/A/S/D keys.

✨ Power-ups

🔥

Rapid Fire

Increases firing rate for 10 seconds

🛡️

Shield

Temporary invincibility for 8 seconds

⭐

Multi-shot

Fire 3 bullets at once for 12 seconds

❤️

Extra Life

Adds one life permanently

💣

Bomb

Clears all enemies on screen instantly

🎯 Scoring System

Destroy small enemy 100 points
Destroy medium enemy 250 points
Destroy large enemy 500 points
Destroy boss 1000 points
Collect power-up 50 points
Collect extra life 200 points

🌟 Levels

The game features 6 unique levels, each ending with a challenging boss battle:

  • Level 1: Asteroid Field - Boss: Rock Destroyer
  • Level 2: Enemy Fleet - Boss: Battle Cruiser
  • Level 3: Alien Planet - Boss: Mothership
  • Level 4: Space Station - Boss: Station Core
  • Level 5: Nebula Storm - Boss: Storm Bringer
  • Level 6: Enemy Base - Boss: Final Boss

❤️ Lives & Game Over

You start with 3 lives (configurable in Settings). When your spaceship collides with enemies or their bullets, you lose a life.

Game Over occurs when all lives are depleted. Your score is saved to the leaderboard!

🎚️ Difficulty Levels

  • 🟢 Easy: Slower enemies, lower spawn rate, simple bullet patterns
  • 🟡 Normal: Balanced challenge for experienced players
  • 🔴 Hard: Fast enemies, high spawn rate, complex bullet patterns
